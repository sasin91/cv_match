What it does for you

The checking, the sorting and the first draft.

the one you'd have scrolled past

Finds the jobs you'd have missed

You search for your last job title. JobFinder reads every new post and judges it against your whole CV, so a "planner" job you never searched for still turns up.

5 of 6, and here's why

Tells you why

Every match lists the post's requirements, which ones you meet and what on your CV shows it. Ten seconds tells you whether it's worth a look.

signed in as you

Reads the sites you sign in to

LinkedIn and Indeed hide many jobs behind a login. JobFinder opens them in its own browser on your PC, signed in as you, slowly and politely.

first draft, done

Starts the application

For any match, it drafts the letter and a résumé tailored to the post. You change what you like and send it yourself.

a-kasse, sorted

Fills in your joblog

On dagpenge? The Jobnet extension takes the job, the date and your PDFs from JobFinder, so the a-kasse paperwork takes seconds.

home, not the cloud

Stays on your PC

It can run its own AI model, so your CV never has to leave your computer. No subscriptions required.

New: the Android app

Your shortlist in your pocket. Your data stays home.

See today's matches on the bus, or send a post you just spotted to your PC to be checked. The phone talks straight to your PC over Tailscale, a free private network for your own devices.

Nothing in the cloud.

There's no JobFinder server. Your CV and matches never leave your PC, and the phone only asks the PC for them. Tailscale introduces the two devices but can't read what they say to each other.

Works away from home.

Not just on your home Wi-Fi: wherever the phone has a connection, it can reach your PC, as long as the PC is on.

Paired with a QR code, once.

The code works one time, for five minutes. After that the phone has its own key. Lose the phone, press Remove, and the key stops working right away.

Never on the open internet.

Only your own devices can reach your PC, and only a paired phone gets an answer.

Questions

Fair questions.

Why would I need this? ChatGPT already does this.

ChatGPT can run a scheduled task that searches for jobs, and that's a fine start. But it searches the web, so it finds what a search engine has indexed, not every post on Jobnet and Jobindex, and it can't sign in to LinkedIn or Indeed as you. Its results arrive as a message, not a list that remembers which jobs you've seen, rejected, applied to and logged. JobFinder reads the sites themselves, keeps that list, scores every post the same way, and can do it all without your CV leaving your PC.

Is my CV sent anywhere?

Only if you choose a cloud model. With JobFinder's own model, your CV, matches and applications stay in one file on your PC. There is no JobFinder server and no account.

Does it apply for me?

No, on purpose. It finds, scores and drafts. You read the draft, change what you want and send it yourself.

Is it free?

Yes, completely. It's open source under the BSD licence, with no paid tier and no ads.
