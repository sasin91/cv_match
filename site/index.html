Job hunting, without the grind.

JobFinder checks the job sites for you while your PC is on, picks out the few that fit your CV, and starts the application.

Download for Windows

Completely free, and runs locally while staying out of your way. No AI subscription required.
Also for Linux: the AppImage. Nothing to install.

What you come back to

Kick back and relax. Let JobFinder handle the chores.

While your PC is on, it reads Jobnet, Jobindex, LinkedIn and Indeed every two hours, quietly, in the background. When you sit down, the few posts worth your time are waiting, each with a reason.

What it does for you

The checking, the sorting and the first draft.

the one you'd have scrolled past

Finds the jobs you'd have missed

You search for your last job title. JobFinder reads every new post and judges it against your whole CV, so a "planner" job you never searched for still turns up.

5 of 6, and here's why

Tells you why

Every match lists the post's requirements, which ones you meet and what on your CV shows it. Ten seconds tells you whether it's worth a look.

first draft, done

Starts the application

For any match, it drafts the letter and a résumé tailored to the post. You change what you like and send it yourself.
