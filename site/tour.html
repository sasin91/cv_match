How it works

Three things it does, so you don't have to.

Clips from the author's own JobFinder, in light or dark to match your system.

Step 1

It reads the job sites.

Every two hours while your PC is on, or when you press Scan now. Jobnet, Jobindex, LinkedIn and Indeed, and the task manager shows what it's reading.

Step 2

It tells you why a job fits.

Each match lists the post's requirements, whether you meet them, and the line from your CV that shows it. Ten seconds tells you whether it's worth your evening.

Step 3

A letter written for this job, not for every job.

It uses what the post asks for and what your CV shows. Ask for another go and the old one is kept, so you can always go back.

And also

The parts you don't see.

signed in as you

Reads the sites you sign in to

LinkedIn and Indeed hide many jobs behind a login. JobFinder opens them in its own browser on your PC, signed in as you, slowly and politely.

a-kasse, sorted

Fills in your joblog

On dagpenge? The Jobnet extension takes the job, the date and your PDFs from JobFinder, so the a-kasse paperwork takes seconds.

home, not the cloud

Stays on your PC

It can run its own AI model, so your CV never has to leave your computer. No subscriptions required.
