Questions

Fair questions.

Why would I need this? ChatGPT already does this.

ChatGPT can run a scheduled task that searches for jobs, and that's a fine start. But it searches the web, so it finds what a search engine has indexed, not every post on Jobnet and Jobindex, and it can't sign in to LinkedIn or Indeed as you. Its results arrive as a message, not a list that remembers which jobs you've seen, rejected, applied to and logged. JobFinder reads the sites themselves, keeps that list, scores every post the same way, and can do it all without your CV leaving your PC.

Is my CV sent anywhere?

Only if you choose a cloud model. With JobFinder's own model, your CV, matches and applications stay in one file on your PC. There is no JobFinder server and no account.

Does it apply for me?

No, on purpose. It finds, scores and drafts. You read the draft, change what you want and send it yourself.

Is it free?

Yes, completely. It's open source under the BSD licence, with no paid tier and no ads.
